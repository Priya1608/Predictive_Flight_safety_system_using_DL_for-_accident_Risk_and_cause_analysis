Introduction:
The absence of an integrated AI-based flight safety prediction system limits the ability to assess accident risk in real time.
Traditional aviation safety systems mainly focus on post-accident investigation rather than proactive accident prevention.
Existing systems fail to efficiently combine flight, human, and environmental data for comprehensive risk analysis.
Lack of real-time predictive analytics reduces the ability to detect critical flight anomalies during approach and landing phases.

Abstract:

The increasing adoption of Artificial Intelligence in aviation safety has enabled the development of intelligent accident prevention systems. This project presents a Predictive Flight Safety System that utilizes deep learning models integrated with Gated Recurrent Units (GRU) and attention mechanisms to analyze multi-source flight data, including QAR, FDR, HFACS human factor reports, and METAR weather data.
The proposed system focuses on the critical approach and landing phases, where a majority of aviation accidents occur. By analyzing flight patterns, environmental conditions, and human factors, the system provides real-time accident risk prediction, cause identification, and proactive safety recommendations.
The integration of explainable and predictive AI techniques enhances pilot decision-making, improves operational safety, and supports modern aviation safety management through efficient and proactive accident prevention strategies.

Propsed methodology:
Predictive Alerting:
The GRU model detects hidden risk patterns early and provides advance alerts to pilots before minor issues become critical accidents.
Deep Learning using GRU:
A GRU-based deep learning model processes flight and weather data in real time to identify dangerous patterns during approach and landing phases.
Risk Identification and Prevention:
The system predicts accident probability and identifies causes such as technical failures, adverse weather conditions, or pilot errors, enabling preventive action before accidents occur.
